Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Nod Arcade (bundle com.nodarcade.ios) is published by Rohit Grover / Atul Das. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Gameplay Data

Scores, settings and progress are stored locally on your device.

Camera & Motion

Camera, device motion and AirPods head‑motion are processed on‑device in real time only to control gameplay. Camera frames and motion data are not recorded, stored or transmitted to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as IDFA, only if you allow tracking via Apple's prompt), IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve, limit and measure ads. See Google's privacy information. You can decline tracking and get non‑personalized ads.

Purchases

Remove Ads is purchased via Apple In‑App Purchase; we never receive your payment details.

No Sale of Data

We do not sell your personal data. Other than the ad SDK processing described above, we do not share it.

Children

Nod Arcade is rated 12+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's data; parents should supervise use by younger users and contact us to request deletion.

Deletion

Deleting the app removes local data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
